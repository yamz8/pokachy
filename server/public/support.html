Get help

Something isn’t working

Open a GitHub issue with your Pokachy version, Linux desktop, and steps to reproduce the problem. Issues are public: omit email addresses, account tokens, sign-in codes, and private logs.

Unwanted contact

Run pokachy block @handle to stop contact. Run pokachy report @handle "Reason for the report" to submit a report to the service administrator. Only accepted friends can poke you.

Private support and appeals

Email support@pokachy.com for private support, privacy requests, or suspension appeals. Include your handle and a brief description. Never send passwords, session tokens, or sign-in codes, and do not publish private reports in GitHub issues.

Your data

Read privacy and retention, manage signed-in devices from your account, or delete your account.

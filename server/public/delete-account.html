Delete your account

This permanently removes your profile, linked sign-ins, friendships, blocks, poke history, and reports involving your account from the active database. All signed-in devices lose access. Your handle becomes available again.

You cannot undo this. Friends lose the shared history on the server too. Recovery copies can retain deleted data for up to 30 days. An email already queued may still arrive, but its deleted sign-in code will no longer work.

Offline computers and desktop notification history cannot be erased remotely. Run pokachy logout on each computer to remove Pokachy’s saved credentials and cache, and clear any desktop notification history separately.
